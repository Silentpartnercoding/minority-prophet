# LIR-1 decisions

## 2026-08-08 — integrate rather than create a separate project

The study is part of Minority Prophet because it directly tests the repository's
declared limitation: real-world provenance recovery is not established.

## 2026-08-08 — separate truth, descent, and independence

The original common schema collapsed different questions into one
`ground_truth_label`. LIR-1 records content truth, record descent, evidence
roots, label basis, and label scope separately. This prevents an observed
retweet or a text-similarity rule from being presented as proof of causal
independence.

## 2026-08-08 — make the LLM corpus primary

Only the generated corpus can provide exact independence labels by
construction at useful scale. Real-world datasets remain essential external
stress tests but are stratified by the strength of their labels.

## 2026-08-08 — keep weak proxies, demote their claims

Retraction-context and prediction-market studies remain in scope because their
failure modes are informative. Their labels are exploratory proxies and do not
enter the primary confirmatory claim.
