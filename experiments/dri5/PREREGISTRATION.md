# DRI-5 — preregistration, protocol v1

**Status: FROZEN, protocol v1, 2026-09-15. NOT RUN.** Frozen before any
confirmatory world is generated or scored. The runner pins this file, the
configuration, the implementation, the reused DRI-4 and DRI-3 files and the engine
by SHA-256, and refuses to run if any of them has changed. When this was frozen, no
comparative outcome had been computed on any DRI-5 world. Development runs checked
construction invariants and runtime only.

Design rationale: `research/decision-relative-independence/DRI-5-DESIGN-DRAFT.md`.
Implementation: `experiments/dri5/`, which reuses the frozen DRI-4 generator and
degradation, and the DRI-3 arms and decision scoring. Related proofs:
`formal/lean/MinorityProphetCore/DependenceRobustness.lean`, ledger DR2 and DR3.

## 1. Identifier

DRI-5, protocol v1.

## 2. Questions

1. **Recovery:** when true shared identities are missing from the lineage record,
   does counting a content fingerprint as possible dependence remove the tiered
   rule's silent false settlements?
2. **Paraphrase and collisions:** does that survive rewording of copies and chance
   collisions between independent sources?
3. **Blind spot:** which silent false settlements remain?

## 3. Hypotheses

- **Null:** counting content makes no difference to silent false settlements once
  lineage is missing.
- **Target:** section 10.
- **Stated expectation:** content removes errors that come from forgotten copies,
  not errors from roots that share a component or origin without being copies. The
  criterion is not narrowed to where this expectation predicts success.

## 4. Generated worlds

- **Families and size.** DRI-4's five families, 2,000 base worlds each, generated by
  the frozen DRI-4 generator on this protocol's own salt:
  - `joint_domain`;
  - `separate_control_shared_origin`;
  - `three_stacked`;
  - `decoy_shared_identity`;
  - `side_asymmetric_trap`.

  Each world has 3 decisions, a lookup in even-numbered worlds, and exactly half of
  the decisions irreversible.
- **Missing lineage.** DRI-4's degradation at m ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5}, with spurious rate
  0. Each identity group that encodes a true dependence is split into distinct
  identities with probability m.
- **Content fingerprint.** Every observation gets an identity at the `content` cut:
  - every copy of a root repeats that root's fingerprint;
  - with paraphrase rate p ∈ {0, 0.5}, each copy after the first gets a fingerprint
    of its own;
  - with collision rate q ∈ {0, 0.2}, a root that has an earlier root on the same side
    reuses the fingerprint of one of those roots, chosen uniformly;
  - roots of one true unit that are not copies get independent fingerprints;
  - content is not degraded by m.
- **Unchanged:** truth, true grouping and lookup.
- **Cells.** 12 cells, m × p × q. Every cell uses the same base worlds.
- **Common random numbers.** One content stream per base world, seeded by SHA-256 of
  `salt|content|family|replicate`, draws every number regardless of the rates. A
  paraphrase or collision at a lower rate is therefore also one at a higher rate, and
  content is identical at every m.
- **Totals.** 10,000 base worlds × 12 cells = 120,000 scored worlds, with 6,000
  decisions per family per cell.
- **Seeds.** Base worlds and degradation are seeded as in DRI-4. The confirmatory
  salt is `minority-prophet-dri5-v1-confirmatory`; development uses
  `minority-prophet-dri5-development` only.

## 5. Arms

- **Frozen DRI-3 arms, unchanged, reading lineage cuts only:**
  - agreement rule;
  - robustness everywhere;
  - tiered rule;
  - always look.
- **Content robustness everywhere:** settles only on a settlement robust over lineage
  and content; otherwise it looks.
- **Content tiered rule (method under test):**
  - **irreversible decisions:** as content robustness everywhere;
  - **reversible decisions:** as the tiered rule, with the "not robust" stamp judged
    over lineage and content. Whether to settle without looking is still decided by
    agreement over the five lineage cuts.
- **Oracle reference.**

There is no human. Lookups are truthful.

## 6. Scoring

Scoring follows DRI-3 (`experiments/dri3/scoring.py::score_decision`):

- correct settlement;
- false settlement, which is **flagged** if stamped "not robust" and **silent**
  otherwise;
- unneeded abstention;
- required abstention.

One definition applies to every arm alike: a decision is decidable from the record
when lineage and content together robustly settle it the way the true grouping does.

Results are kept per family and per cell and are never pooled. Time is measured,
not a criterion.

## 7. Endpoints

- **Primary, per family and cell:** silent false settlements by the content tiered
  rule and the tiered rule, and the paired comparison between them.
- **Reported, per family and cell:**
  - **admissibility:** decisions whose true grouping is admissible over lineage, and
    over lineage and content;
  - **recoverable errors:** the tiered rule's silent false settlements on decisions
    where content restores admissibility;
  - **every arm:** every outcome count, looks, stamped settlements and stamped
    correct settlements;
  - **irreversible:** false settlements for both tiered arms.

## 8. Uncertainty and multiple testing

- **Zero-event bound.** With zero events in 6,000 decisions, the exact one-sided 95%
  upper bound is 0.000499.
- **Paired test.** Exact McNemar on paired per-decision silent-false-settlement
  indicators, with Holm correction at a family-wise α of 0.05 across every powered
  comparison.
- **When a comparison is run.** At most 40 comparisons are possible: 5 families × 8
  cells with m ∈ {0.25, 0.5}. A comparison runs only when the tiered rule makes at
  least 12 silent false settlements in that family and cell. Twelve lets an exact test
  with no discordance on the other side reach significance at the strictest Holm step
  (2 × 0.5¹² < 0.05 / 40). Otherwise the comparison is reported as underpowered and is
  not a check.

## 9. Invalidation

- the runner detects a changed hash;
- the two executions differ;
- a contestant arm is shown to have read a hidden field;
- a content fingerprint is shared across sides.

## 10. Success criterion

Supported only if all of the following hold:

1. **Complete lineage (m = 0):** in every family and cell, the content tiered rule
   makes zero silent false settlements, with the 95% upper bound below 0.001.
2. **Implementation of the guarantees:** in every family and cell, the content tiered
   rule makes:
   - no silent false settlement on a decision where the tiered rule does not;
   - no silent false settlement on a decision whose true grouping is admissible over
     lineage and content.
3. **Recovery (m ∈ {0.25, 0.5}):** in every powered comparison (section 8), the
   content tiered rule makes significantly fewer silent false settlements than the
   tiered rule.
4. **Reproducibility:** two complete executions give identical semantic results.

Items 1 and 2 check the implementation of consequences of DR2 and of monotonicity;
they are not discoveries. Item 3 is the finding.
