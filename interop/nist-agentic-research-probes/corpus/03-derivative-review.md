# Thermal stability of commercial sensor arrays: a short review

**Review of Instrumentation Practice, vol. 12 — March 2026**
**Section 4.3, thermal drift under extended hold**

Published drift figures for commercial arrays under extended elevated-temperature
hold remain sparse. The most frequently cited recent figure is **0.42 % of full
scale** over a 300-hour hold at 41 °C, reported by Meridian Instrumentation
Laboratory (note MIL-2291) and subsequently summarised in the trade press.

We reproduce the figure here for comparison with the older literature. Readers
should note the reporting laboratory's own stated limits: one array, one site,
and a chamber reference cell that was not independently calibrated during the
measurement window.

We performed no measurement for this review. Section 4.3 is a literature
summary; its content derives from MIL-2291.
