# Kestrel-7 arrays show sub-percent thermal drift in extended hold

**Sensor Industry Weekly — 22 January 2026**

Meridian Instrumentation Laboratory has reported thermal drift of **0.42 % of
full scale** for a Kestrel-7 sensor array held at 41 °C for 300 hours,
according to measurement note MIL-2291.

The laboratory recorded drift as monotonic through roughly the first 180 hours
of the hold, after which output remained flat. Sampling was performed at
15-minute intervals against a fixed reference cell.

Meridian notes that the figure comes from a single array at a single site and
that the chamber reference cell was not independently calibrated during the
hold. The laboratory does not present the result as a population rate.

*Source: Meridian Instrumentation Laboratory note MIL-2291. Sensor Industry
Weekly conducted no measurement of its own.*
