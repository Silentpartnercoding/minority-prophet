# Thermal drift in the Kestrel-7 sensor array: single-site measurement

**Meridian Instrumentation Laboratory — internal measurement note MIL-2291**
**Recorded 14 January 2026. Single site, single array.**

## What was measured

One Kestrel-7 sensor array, serial K7-0442, was held at 41 °C for 300 hours in
the Meridian environmental chamber. Output drift was sampled every 15 minutes
against a fixed reference cell.

## Result

Mean drift over the 300-hour hold was **0.42 %** of full scale (n = 1 array,
1200 samples). Drift was monotonic for the first 180 hours and flat thereafter.

## Stated limits

This is one array at one temperature at one site. The chamber's own reference
cell was not independently calibrated during the hold. No second array was run.
Nothing here establishes a population rate, a temperature dependence, or a
failure threshold. A single unit does not characterise a production lot.

*This note is the only measurement. Documents 02 and 03 in this corpus report
this same measurement and contain no independent observation.*
