# LIR-2/PHEME transfer reproduction

The transfer protocol and frozen scorer were committed before the LIR-2 grouper
was executed on the private PHEME-R2 normalized claims. Threshold `0.75` was
carried over unchanged from the constructed LIR-2 development result.

Two executions produced byte-identical result files with SHA-256
`579db4ef73c08feddd235eebfaad0a40b4fe63aa238020dd550ae4e13c3bd73b`.
The private normalized input is bound by SHA-256
`1c3e9e08149021cdb81da02b96750d75e0f0dce1dd7432bf5f7613fb206a2266`.

The corpus had been studied previously by LIR-1/PHEME-R2, so this is a
prospective method-transfer result on a known corpus, not an independent new
dataset validation.
