4. LINEAGE-BEARING SCHEMA, AND END-TO-END TESTS FOR THEOREM 1 AND LEMMA 1

Theorem 1 (immunity to root-preserving, side-consistent rewiring) and Lemma 1
(side-locality over claim chains) are untested because schema v0.1 records carry
only {id, rootId, side}. There are no parent edges, so every claim is its own
root and both claims are vacuous in the model.

Design the minimal extension that makes them testable: records carrying a
parent edge, with root() derived by walking to the chain head. Register it as
its own schema version; do NOT modify schema v0.1 or any KL-000 registration.

Then build tests that could actually fail:

  - Theorem 1: generate a world, apply a rewiring that preserves the root set
    and side-consistency, assert S0, S1 and the verdict are unchanged. Include
    rewirings that VIOLATE each precondition separately and assert the theorem's
    protection does not extend to them -- a test that only exercises the
    satisfied case cannot fail.
  - Lemma 1: assert S_a is exactly the set of a-asserting roots in a
    side-consistent world, and construct a side-INCONSISTENT world where it
    does not hold.

Exhaustive over a declared small bound plus a frozen randomized phase, in the
KL-000 style: declared count asserted before evaluation, seeds frozen,
must-fail baselines. If you cannot reach a million worlds cheaply, declare the
bound you did reach rather than quietly shrinking.
