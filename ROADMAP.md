# Public research roadmap

## Established

- Formal assumptions, counterexamples, and compiler-ratified core proofs.
- Canonical synthetic records, archived-implementation replays, and preserved
  rejected or incomplete results.
- Bounded authenticated root issuance and adversarial conformance tests.
- A sanitized field observation of root identity and observer concentration.
- A preregistered selective-challenger experiment (EXP009).
- A closed LIR-1–LIR-4 lineage-inference series identifying typed provenance as
  a load-bearing recorded-lineage signal and substantial identity missingness
  as a fragmentation boundary.

Repository acceptance remains simple: a clean checkout passes its tests and
reproduces seeded records offline.

## Next

1. Complete the market matched-coverage analysis.
2. Compare against released truth-discovery implementations.
3. Verify every paper citation against its primary source.
4. Test the neutral evidence contract against a real provider.
5. Define and test root identity, shared dependencies, time, revocation, and
   key-compromise boundaries without treating identity as independence.
6. Continue the dual evidence/search ledger program in
   [`RESEARCH-DIRECTION.md`](RESEARCH-DIRECTION.md), including controls that
   prevent incomplete coverage from becoming an absence claim.

The LIR series is closed. A future LIR-5 is optional and separate: it must use
a balanced multi-root adversarial corpus or a genuinely different platform with
strong lineage labels. It must not reuse PHEME volume as a substitute for the
missing cross-root safety evidence.

These are research tasks, not product claims. Current status is controlled by
[`CANONICAL-RECORDS.md`](CANONICAL-RECORDS.md) and
[`EVIDENCE-ALIGNMENT.md`](EVIDENCE-ALIGNMENT.md).

Confirmatory experiments retain grounded-majority, ungrounded-minority, and
corrupted-lineage controls.
